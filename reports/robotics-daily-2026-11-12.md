### [AHEADance: Anticipatory Humanoid Dance from Streaming Audio](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13370) — 作者:Tianhu Peng, Yukun Wang, Lingfan Bao, Yiyang Jia 等
🏷️ 交叉方向 / Also: 运动生成 / Motion Generation(动作生成器 + 全身跟踪器在真机执行,按规则归入运动控制)
开源状态 / Code: 🕒 计划开源 / Planned — [项目页](https://ihcr.github.io/AHEADance)上的代码按钮标注 "Code soon",目前没有仓库链接。
- 🇨🇳 人形机器人跟着流式音乐跳舞时,必须在听到某段音乐之前就提交对应的动作块,并且要接着已经提交的动作继续跳;离线舞蹈生成方法做不到这两点。AHEADance 是一个面向机器人的流式舞蹈生成器,生成结果由全身跟踪器(whole-body tracker)在人形机器人上执行。其中 ListenAhead 模块让每个动作块同时以已听到的音乐和冻结音乐模型对后续音乐的预测为条件,预测按目标时间对齐并按可靠性加权;Commit Forcing 则让模型从自己已提交的动作块继续训练,同时更新潜在历史和下一块的解码边界。结果显示,音乐预测携带的节拍时序几乎与真实未来音乐一样好,生成动作与音乐更同步,运动能量更集中在节拍频率上;Commit Forcing 把"低活动"比例从 83.5% 降到 10.3%,与真人舞蹈水平相当。在 Unitree G1 真机上,机器人随音乐起舞,音乐停止即停下,全程无摔倒。
- 🇬🇧 A humanoid dancing to streamed music must commit each motion chunk before hearing the music it accompanies, and must continue from motion it has already committed, which offline dance generators do not handle. AHEADance is a robot-native streaming dance generator whose output is executed on the humanoid by a whole-body tracker. ListenAhead conditions each chunk on heard music plus a frozen music model's forecasts of upcoming music, aligned to target times and weighted by reliability; Commit Forcing trains continuation from the model's own committed chunks, updating both the latent history and the decoded boundary of the next chunk. The forecasts carry upcoming beat timing almost as well as the true future, improving synchronization and concentrating motion energy at the beat frequency, and Commit Forcing reduces low activity from 83.5% to 10.3%, the level of real dance. On a physical Unitree G1 the robot dances while music plays and comes to rest when it stops, without a fall.

### [ReACT: Reflection-Consistent Modulation and Cross-Load Consistency for Humanoid Velocity Tracking Under Asymmetric Loads](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13929) — 作者:Xianghui Wang, Aoran Fu, Liyou Luo, Shanze Wang 等
🏷️ 交叉方向 / Also: 人形 loco-manipulation / Humanoid Loco-Manipulation
开源状态 / Code: ❓ 未知 / Unknown — Comments 为空,摘要没有提到代码或项目页。
- 🇨🇳 人形 loco-manipulation 已经发展到全身抓取、举起和搬运,而把物体送到目的地的前提是运动控制器能准确执行导航策略给出的速度指令;单手持物带来持续的上身不对称负载,会让速度跟踪出现偏差,并累积成位置和航向误差,增加碰撞和导航失败的风险。ReACT 提出两个设计。一是"镜像一致扰动调制"(reflection-consistent disturbance modulation):用估计的外力/力矩调制 actor 特征来补偿负载,同时保证镜像观测下的动作对应关系不变。二是"跨负载跟踪一致性"训练:在相同速度指令下比较有载和无载两种条件,促使两者的速度跟踪保持一致。Unitree G1 仿真实验显示,在不同不对称负载下速度跟踪更准、方向偏差更小;真机上用固定的无地图导航策略,单手负载下 40 次导航试验全部无碰撞到达目标。
- 🇬🇧 Humanoid loco-manipulation now covers whole-body grasping, lifting and transport, which all require the locomotion controller to execute navigation velocity commands accurately; a one-handed load creates a persistent asymmetric upper-body disturbance whose tracking bias accumulates into position and heading errors. ReACT uses reflection-consistent disturbance modulation, where the estimated wrench modulates actor features for load compensation while preserving action correspondence under mirrored observations, together with cross-load tracking-consistency training that compares loaded and unloaded conditions under the same commands. Unitree G1 simulations show better velocity tracking and less directional bias across asymmetric loads. On hardware, a fixed mapless navigation policy reached its goal without collision in all 40 trials under unilateral hand loads.

### [RedirectMimic: Local Motion Content Redaction for Humanoid Whole-Body Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14049) — 作者:Zepeng Wang, Xiaochuan Shi
开源状态 / Code: ❓ 未知 / Unknown — [项目页](https://xiaopeng-whu.github.io/RedirectMimic/)只有 BibTeX 等内容,没有代码链接或开源承诺。
- 🇨🇳 人形动作接口需要满足各种应用层面的动作约束(例如禁止某些手臂动作),同时保留参考动作中有用的部分。所谓"局部内容删改",就是控制器在仍然收到原始参考的情况下,把选定的动作替换掉;如果只是去掉这些动作的跟踪奖励,替代动作就无从约束。RedirectMimic 把 actor 观察到的参考和训练执行时用的目标分开:PPO 训练中用规则生成的编辑后参考来计算跟踪奖励,而部署时的观测和动作接口保持不变。作者在 Unitree G1 仿真中,基于一个用 7,509 段动作预训练的跟踪器,评估了按姿态和按速度定义的手臂编辑。在"举手过头"任务中,核心窗口内违禁内容的出现率降到 2.7%(奖励置零为 58.9%,反跟踪为 32.8%),核心窗口关节成功率 94.2%,同时做到内容抑制、无摔倒执行和局部保留;它在各方法中替换误差最低,对未编辑动作的跟踪精度与预训练控制器相当。
- 🇬🇧 Humanoid motion interfaces should honor application-specific movement constraints while keeping useful reference behavior; local content redaction means replacing selected movements even though the controller still receives the original reference, and simply removing the tracking reward leaves the replacement unspecified. RedirectMimic separates the reference observed by the actor from the target used to train execution: a rule-generated edited reference drives PPO tracking rewards while the deployed observation and action interfaces stay unchanged. In Unitree G1 simulation with a tracker pretrained on 7,509 clips, the overhead-arm task sees core-window content occurrence drop to 2.7% (vs. 58.9% for reward zeroing and 32.8% for anti-tracking), with 94.2% core-window joint success. It also has the lowest replacement error among the editing methods and tracks unedited motion as accurately as the pretrained controller.

### [Koopman Model-Based Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14186) — 作者:Wenjian Hao, Paulo C. Heredia, Shaoshuai Mou
🏷️ 交叉方向 / Also: 运动控制·传统 / Classical(Koopman 线性化模型;按 RL + 模型混合规则归入 RL)
开源状态 / Code: ❓ 未知 / Unknown — Comments 为空。
- 🇨🇳 对于动力学未知的非线性机器人系统,无模型 RL 样本效率低,基于模型的 RL 又会因多步 rollout 累积预测误差。K-MBRL 用随机 Koopman 算子学习,把未知非线性动力学近似为线性表示。策略目标由解析的即时代价加上 critic 在单步预测状态上估计的未来代价组成:线性 Koopman 动力学让依赖模型的策略梯度简化为常数矩阵乘积,计算高效;单步模型传播既避免了长时域误差累积,又通过 critic 保留了长期代价信息。学到的策略只需前馈计算即可实时控制,不需要在线优化;作者在标准随机逼近假设下证明了收敛性。在 HalfCheetah 和四足运动仿真上,样本效率和性能都优于经典无模型、基于模型的 RL 基线;真机上,四足机器人完成了"靠两只圆形后脚站立、保持平衡双足行走、再安全回到地面"的高难度动作序列(摘要没有给出更多定量结果)。
- 🇬🇧 For nonlinear robots with unknown dynamics, model-free RL is sample-hungry while model-based RL accumulates multi-step prediction errors. K-MBRL learns a stochastic Koopman operator that represents the dynamics linearly and optimizes a policy objective combining the analytic immediate cost with a critic's estimate of future cost at the one-step-predicted state. Linear Koopman dynamics reduce the model-dependent policy gradient to a constant matrix product, and one-step propagation avoids long-horizon error accumulation while the critic retains long-term cost; the policy runs feedforward with no online optimization, and convergence is proven under standard stochastic-approximation assumptions. It beats classical model-free and model-based baselines in sample efficiency on HalfCheetah and quadruped locomotion, and on hardware a quadruped rises onto two rounded rear feet, walks bipedally while balancing, and returns safely to the ground (no further quantitative results in the abstract).

### [DexSeed: Expanding Human Hand-Object Interactions into Robot Demonstrations for Dexterous Policy Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14092) — 作者:Huaijin Pi, Zehong Shen, Yong Wang, Kechun Xu 等(HKU、Robbyant/Ant Group、浙江大学)
🏷️ 交叉方向 / Also: VLA, 具身数据 / Embodied Data(RL 手指控制策略生成可执行示范,按规则归入运动控制)
开源状态 / Code: 🕒 计划开源 / Planned — 官方仓库 [robbyant-research/DexSeed](https://github.com/robbyant-research/DexSeed) 匿名验证存在,但目前只有 README 和素材,README 写明 "Code release: Coming soon";[项目页](https://robbyant-research.github.io/DexSeed/)。
- 🇨🇳 为灵巧 VLA 生成多样且物理可执行的机器人示范,通常依赖昂贵的遥操作;人手-物体交互数据集可以复用,但现有迁移方法大多只能复现给定的交互,难以覆盖不同物体、不同操作位置,也难以生成可执行的手臂和手指动作及视觉观测。DexSeed 的思路是在保留人类抓取方式的前提下扩展人类序列,并在物理仿真中用手臂跟踪动力学把扩展后的运动执行出来。具体做法:按抓取模式组织源序列,并配上共享的语言描述;用基于 InteractionMesh 的重定向和手臂轨迹增强,把序列适配到机器人手、不同物体几何和不同放置位置,同时保留局部手-物关系;由 **RL 手指控制策略**生成可执行 rollout,手臂则跟踪预先计算的轨迹;成功 rollout 的渲染观测和记录动作与抓取描述配对,用于微调 VLA。实验显示,其重定向的接触保真度更高,整臂执行比对比基线更可靠;只用生成示范微调的 VLA 能在仿真和真机上执行语言指定的抓取(摘要没有给出具体数值)。
- 🇬🇧 Generating diverse, physically executable demonstrations for dexterous VLAs usually needs costly teleoperation, and existing human-to-robot transfer mostly replays given interactions without covering new objects, locations, executable arm/finger actions or observations. DexSeed expands human hand–object sequences while preserving their grasps: sequences are grouped by grasp mode with shared language descriptions, retargeted with InteractionMesh plus arm-trajectory augmentation to new robot hands, object geometries and pick-and-place locations, then executed in physics simulation where an RL finger-control policy produces rollouts while the arm tracks precomputed trajectories. Rendered observations and actions from successful rollouts, paired with grasp descriptions, fine-tune a VLA. Retargeting shows higher contact fidelity and more reliable whole-arm execution than the baselines, and a VLA trained only on generated data executes language-specified grasps in simulation and on a real robot (no specific numbers in the abstract).

### [ExRL: Sample-Efficient Online Adaptation of Long-Chunk Policies via Execution Length Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13482) — 作者:Xu Luo, Jiaying Yang, Hao Wu, Junlin Xie 等
🏷️ 交叉方向 / Also: VLA(长动作块策略的在线 RL 适配)
开源状态 / Code: ✅ 已开源 / Released — [Frankluox/ExRL](https://github.com/Frankluox/ExRL);来源是 HF Daily Papers 的 githubRepo 字段,匿名 `git ls-remote` 验证存在。
- 🇨🇳 预测长动作块(往往跨几十个时间步)已成为强机器人策略的关键要素,但直接用现有 RL 方法适配这类策略,需要优化的动作块维度很高,所需在线样本很多,在真实交互数据昂贵的场景中不现实。作者的关键想法是:把适配对象从"动作本身"换成"每个预测动作块执行多长"。这样适配空间保持低维,而表达力会随块长增加而增长。ExRL(Execution at the Right Length)给基础策略加了一个轻量适配器,预测每个块的执行长度,并用离策略(off-policy)RL 训练。在仿真基准和真实机器人任务上,ExRL 只需极少的任务特定超参数调节,就能样本高效地适配多种长动作块策略(摘要没有给出具体数值)。
- 🇬🇧 Long action chunks spanning dozens of timesteps are now central to capable robot policies, but adapting them with standard RL needs many online samples because the optimized chunks are high-dimensional, which is impractical when real interaction is costly. ExRL shifts adaptation from optimizing actions to optimizing how long each predicted chunk is executed, which keeps the adaptation space low-dimensional while its expressivity grows with chunk size. A lightweight adapter predicts the execution length of each chunk and is trained with off-policy RL. Across simulation benchmarks and real-robot tasks, ExRL adapts a range of long-chunk policies sample-efficiently with minimal task-specific tuning (the abstract gives no specific numbers).

开源状态 / Code: 🕒 计划开源 / Planned — 摘要称代码见 [haiyang2022/HiPFuse](https://github.com/haiyang2022/HiPFuse),仓库匿名可访问,但目前只有一行 README,没有代码。
- 🇨🇳 实时机器人感知需要在有限资源下得到准确的激光雷达语义,鸟瞰图(BEV)聚合虽然高效,却可能把同一列中标签不同的表面混在一起。HiPFuse 是点-BEV 融合框架,在多个水平尺度上交换点特征和按高度分区的 BEV 特征,把单帧扫描的逐点细节和二维空间上下文结合起来:固定的高度分配保留独立的垂直聚合组,并用同一 3D 单元的目标对初始 BEV 特征做辅助监督;双尺度 BEV 上下文模块再从更粗的栅格引入更大范围的上下文。在 SemanticKITTI 和 MCD 上,它在 Jetson AGX Orin 上分别以 14.2 和 22.4 FPS 取得 63.7% 和 62.5% mIoU,超过 10 Hz 扫描频率;在固定的机器人系统中只替换语义前端,两段室外序列的里程计绝对轨迹误差 RMSE 平均降低 10.6%,SemanticKITTI 上被接受的回环召回率提高 8.7 个百分点。
- 🇬🇧 Real-time LiDAR semantics under tight compute often uses BEV aggregation, which can mix differently labeled surfaces within a column. HiPFuse exchanges point features and height-partitioned BEV features across horizontal scales, keeps separate vertical aggregation groups with auxiliary supervision from the same 3D cells, and adds a dual-scale BEV context module. It reaches 63.7% and 62.5% mIoU on SemanticKITTI and MCD at 14.2 and 22.4 FPS on Jetson AGX Orin, above the 10 Hz scan rate. Swapping only the semantic front end in fixed robotic systems reduces odometry ATE RMSE by 10.6% on average over two outdoor sequences and raises accepted-loop recall by 8.7 points on SemanticKITTI.

### [NIF-VIO: Visual--Inertial Odometry Across Inertial and Non-Inertial Reference Frames](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13662) — 作者:Zichao Shu, Lijun Li, Zhuochen Li, Zetao Chen 等
开源状态 / Code: ❓ 未知 / Unknown — Comments 为空。
- 🇨🇳 传统视觉-惯性里程计(VIO)假设相机和 IMU 的观测在惯性参考系中一致,但在加速或旋转的载体内部(如行驶车辆、船舶舱内),视觉观测反映的是相对载体的运动,IMU 测量却包含额外的载体运动分量,常规融合会因此失效。NIF-VIO 面向跨惯性/非惯性参考系、相对局部环境的定位,根据测量一致性自适应调整视觉-惯性融合:惯性条件变化时,在确认不一致之前就隔离早期约束并做局部重优化,限制误差传播并修复受影响的局部几何;在非惯性条件下,引入参数在时间上共享的紧凑模型来解释载体运动,同时保留与视觉约束互补的惯性信息。公开基准和自采数据集实验显示,它在惯性条件下性能有竞争力,在非惯性条件下定位精度和可用性更好(摘要没有给出具体数值)。
- 🇬🇧 Conventional VIO assumes camera and IMU observations agree in an inertial frame, which breaks inside accelerating or rotating carriers where vision sees carrier-relative motion while the IMU includes carrier-induced components. NIF-VIO localizes relative to the local environment across both regimes by adapting fusion to measurement consistency: when inertial conditions change, early constraint isolation and local reoptimization limit error propagation and repair local geometry before inconsistency is confirmed. Under non-inertial conditions, a compact model with temporally shared parameters accounts for carrier motion while keeping useful inertial information. It is competitive in inertial settings and more accurate and available in non-inertial ones on public and self-collected data (no figures in the abstract).

### [Differentiable Filtering for Inertial Odometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13517) — 作者:Yaru Gu, Ting Zou
开源状态 / Code: 🕒 计划开源 / Planned — 摘要写 "Source code will be made publicly available"。
- 🇨🇳 惯性里程计会因积分传感器噪声和偏置而无界漂移;TLIO 等方法中的神经伪测量是独立于下游滤波器训练的。DFIO 把误差状态卡尔曼滤波器的更新阶段直接嵌入计算图:先单独预训练,再通过可微的测量更新,用后验位置监督联合优化位移头和协方差头。在含已标定和未标定惯性数据的公开行人运动数据集上,DFIO 的平均绝对平移误差和一秒相对平移误差比 TLIO 分别降低 13.2% 和 39.6%,比自身的预训练版本分别降低 7.7% 和 17.4%;在未标定的智能手机惯性数据上,它在高噪声条件下也能有效抑制平移漂移并保持轨迹对齐。
- 🇬🇧 Inertial odometry drifts without bound, and in approaches like TLIO the neural pseudo-measurements are trained separately from the filter. DFIO embeds the error-state Kalman filter update in the computational graph and, after standalone pretraining, jointly optimizes displacement and covariance heads with posterior-position supervision through differentiable updates. On a pedestrian dataset it cuts mean absolute and one-second relative translation errors by 13.2% and 39.6% versus TLIO, and by 7.7% and 17.4% versus its pretrained version. On uncalibrated smartphone IMU data it mitigates drift and keeps trajectory alignment under heavy noise.

### [Persistent Metric-Semantic Maritime Scene Memory from Open-Vocabulary 3D Scene Graphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13903) — 作者:Kim Alexander Christensen, Dina Zimmermann, Martin Steinert, Eleni Kelasidi(NTNU 团队)
开源状态 / Code: ❓ 未知 / Unknown — Comments 给出的[网站](https://kimachristensen.github.io/maritime_3dsg)目前返回 404,没有找到代码。
- 🇨🇳 三维场景图(3DSG)能为机器人提供持久的度量-语义表示用于空间推理,但现有方法主要面向室内和陆地环境。作者研究一种代表性的户外开放词汇方法迁移到海事环境的效果,以及需要哪些适配:零样本使用时,对大多数有几何支撑的物体,描述与人工标注真值一致,但地图存在严重的物理实体碎片化,其面向陆地的高层假设也不符合海事空间组织。因此作者保留底层建图方法,对其累积证据做后处理:先做度量-语义合并,再用基于几何的多视角 VLM 推理把剩余碎片合并为物理实体,并把实体按观测分组为局部"地点";同时利用地图先验和机载的水体/结构观测构建海事宏观区域,并与局部地点关联。所得表示的实体碎片化低于基线和仅做几何-语义合并的版本,支持多次海上航行之间固定地点的对应,并在审阅过的查询上比测试基线有更好的上下文检索(摘要没有给出具体数值)。
- 🇬🇧 3D scene graphs give robots persistent metric-semantic memory, but methods target indoor and terrestrial settings. Applying a representative outdoor open-vocabulary method zero-shot to maritime data gives mostly correct object descriptions but heavy physical-entity fragmentation and terrestrial higher-level assumptions that do not fit maritime layout. The authors keep the mapper and post-process its evidence: metric-semantic consolidation plus geometry-grounded multi-view VLM reasoning merge fragments into entities grouped into local places, and macro regions are built from map priors and onboard water/structure observations. The result reduces fragmentation beyond the baseline and consolidation alone, keeps fixed places consistent across repeated traversals, and improves contextual retrieval on reviewed queries (no figures in the abstract).

3. **人形全身跟踪器成为可编辑、可组合的"平台层"。** [RedirectMimic](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14049) 在不改部署接口的前提下删改参考动作,[AHEADance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13370) 把流式舞蹈生成器接到跟踪器上在 G1 真机运行,[ReACT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13929) 为持物导航补上负载鲁棒的速度跟踪;同时 [SuperMimic 官方代码](https://github.com/HybridRobotics/supermimic)及其 mjlab 依赖全部公开,一天涨了约 900 star。 / Humanoid trackers are becoming an editable platform layer (RedirectMimic, AHEADance, ReACT), as SuperMimic's full code stack goes public and gains ~900 stars in a day.
4. **把人类数据(第一人称视频、手-物交互、游戏)变成机器人训练信号。** [Ego2Robot 综述](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13610)、[Ego Guidance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13685)、[DexSeed](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14092)、[游戏预训练 InternVLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13819),以及用意图空间跨具身的 [CIALLO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13732),都在用不带机器人动作的人类数据换取可扩展性。 / Human data without robot actions (egocentric video, hand–object interaction, gameplay) is being converted into robot training signal.
5. **GPU 上的优化与仿真基础设施。** [FAME-QP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13766) 用 CUDA graph 加速 MPC 的 QP,[Newton 1.8](https://github.com/newton-physics/newton/releases/tag/v1.8.0) 推出面向接触密集 RL 的 Steel 求解器,[MuJoCo 4.0.1](https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco/releases/tag/4.0.1) 修复稀疏 Cholesky 回归,[Discrete MBD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13382) 把扩散采样用于离散组合优化。 / GPU optimization and simulation infrastructure: FAME-QP, Newton's Steel solver, the MuJoCo 4.0.1 fix, and discrete model-based diffusion.

**建议观察 / Watch**
2. **`action chunk`** — 适用:arXiv 检索。理由:多篇论文把长动作块本身(执行长度、检索复用、采样步数)当作研究对象,但目前分散在 VLA 诊断类工作中,信号还需再看几天。 / Several papers treat action chunks themselves as the object of study; worth watching a few more days. 证据 / Evidence:[ExRL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13482)、[How Much of Your VLA Is kNN](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13160)、[Euler Sampling for Flow Matching Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13311)。
3. **`BEHAVIOR Challenge` / `BEHAVIOR-1K`** — 适用:arXiv 检索。理由:CoRL 2026 挑战赛的技术报告正在陆续上线(今日 2 篇),对比 WAM 与 VLA 路线很有参考价值,但属于短期事件。 / Challenge reports comparing WAM and VLA routes are arriving; a short-lived event. 证据 / Evidence:[第 2 名 WAM 方案](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13818)、[第 9 名 VLA 方案](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14432)。

**建议移除/收窄 / Remove or Narrow**
4. **`world model`、`spatial intelligence`、`3D reconstruction`(不加限定)** — 理由:今日 world model 8 条命中中有 3 条无关(蛋白质进化、LLM 符号推理、能量模型综述),spatial intelligence 唯一命中是听觉表征,3D reconstruction 2 条中有 1 条是细胞显微重建;建议加上 `AND (robot OR embodied OR navigation OR manipulation)`。 / These unqualified terms mostly return off-domain hits today; add a robot/embodied/navigation/manipulation qualifier. 证据 / Evidence:[ProTEUS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13348)、[AURA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13687)、[Astrocyte 3D Reconstruction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13581)。
5. **`motion tracking`、`motion imitation`、`robot foundation model`(短语检索)** — 理由:今日均为 0 命中,但 cs.RO 列表中有 [RedirectMimic](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14049)(动作跟踪)和 [AHEADance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13370)(全身跟踪器);短语过严,建议改为 `abs:tracking AND abs:humanoid`、`abs:mimic` 等组合(延续昨日对 RL 短语的同类建议)。 / Zero hits despite relevant list entries; use looser combinations such as `abs:tracking AND abs:humanoid` or `abs:mimic`.
