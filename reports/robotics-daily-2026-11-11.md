### [Kimodo-Policy: Learning Kinematic Action Models for Humanoid Loco-Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11554) — 作者:Yunheng Wang, Jiaxi Zhang, Wei Lu, Yuetong Fang 等
🏷️ 交叉方向 / Also: VLA, 具身基础模型 / Embodied Foundation Models
开源状态 / Code: ✅ 已开源 / Released — [代码仓库](https://github.com/kimodo-policy/kimodo-policy);[项目页](https://kimodo-policy.github.io/)链接,匿名 `git ls-remote` 验证存在;README 勾选了预训练、HumanoidArena 与 SIMPLE 训练/评测、真机数据采集/训练/部署,并提供 [HF 权重](https://huggingface.co/YunhengWang/kimodo-policy);BiGym 2.0 与 HumanoidToolBench 部分标注 coming soon。
- 🇨🇳 人形 loco-manipulation 需要持续协调高维全身运动,而 VLA 与世界-动作模型(WAM)迁移的是语义与视觉动力学先验,未必刻画机器人运动本身的结构。作者提出"运动学动作模型"(Kinematic Action Models, KAMs)范式:把全身运动作为直接预训练目标,用预训练的运动生成器作为动作生成主干,再通过任务条件、视觉观测和执行反馈对其接地。实例 Kimodo-Policy 冻结 Kimodo 文本到动作主干,用轻量 ControlNet 式视觉条件分支,配合"根-身体-手"层级、运动历史条件与重复动作块预测,把动作合成接入闭环控制。大规模 loco-manipulation 预训练后,在多任务 HumanoidArena 上取得对比方法中最高的 72.14% 总体成功率,在 SIMPLE 上具有竞争力,8 项真机任务平均成功率 60%,总参数 678M(可训练 92M);消融支持预训练运动主干的贡献。
- 🇬🇧 Humanoid loco-manipulation needs continuous coordination of high-dimensional whole-body motion, but VLAs and world action models transfer semantic and visual-dynamics priors that do not necessarily capture robot motion structure. The paper proposes Kinematic Action Models (KAMs), which treat whole-body motion as the direct pretraining target and use a pretrained motion generator as the action backbone, grounded by task conditioning, vision and execution feedback. Kimodo-Policy freezes the Kimodo text-to-motion backbone, adds lightweight ControlNet visual conditioning, a root-body-hand hierarchy, motion-history conditioning and repeated action-chunk prediction for closed-loop control. After large-scale pretraining it reaches the best overall success on multi-task HumanoidArena (72.14%), is competitive on SIMPLE, and averages 60% on eight real-world tasks with 678M parameters (92M trainable).

### [SimDuoReal: Object-Centric Bimanual Policy for Emergent Dexterity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10410) — 作者:Qiyang Yan, Yulin Liu, Akash Sharma, Haozhi Qi 等
开源状态 / Code: 🕒 计划开源 / Planned — [项目页](https://simduoreal.github.io)显示 "Code coming soon",无仓库链接。
- 🇨🇳 双臂灵巧操作需要手臂与手的协调,但人工指定这种协调既困难又依赖具体任务。作者探索能否只用简单的以物体为中心的目标让复杂双手灵巧性自然涌现:在共享的物体位姿目标奖励下训练 RL 策略,只改变训练环境而不为每种行为单独调奖励。策略自发学会预抓取操作、抓取与重抓、手内调整和左右手交接;随后在过渡状态上精炼专家,并用 DAgger 与 RL 蒸馏为单一策略以组合这些行为。真机上同一策略在四项任务、未见物体、无任务微调条件下 80 次试验成功 61 次(76.3%);仿真条码扫描任务成功率 85.0%,而无交接的两个单臂策略为 60.4%。
- 🇬🇧 Bimanual dexterity requires coordinated arm and hand control that is hard and task-specific to specify by hand. SimDuoReal trains RL policies under a shared object-centric pose-goal reward, varying training environments instead of tuning per-behavior rewards, and finds that pre-grasp manipulation, grasping/regrasping, in-hand adjustment and handover emerge on their own. Experts are refined on transition states and distilled with DAgger and RL into a single policy that composes these behaviors. On hardware the policy succeeds in 61 of 80 trials (76.3%) across four tasks with unseen objects and no task-specific fine-tuning, and reaches 85.0% on simulated barcode scanning versus 60.4% for two single-arm policies without handover.

### [Efficient Learning of Differentiable MPC Policies on the GPU via Smoothed ADMM and First-Order Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09937) — 作者:Jianghan Zhang, Roland Schwan, Gabriel Bravo-Palacios, John Zhang 等
🏷️ 交叉方向 / Also: 运动控制·传统 / Classical(MPC 求解器;按 RL + MPC 混合规则归入 RL 类)
开源状态 / Code: ✅ 已开源 / Released — [TurboMPC](https://github.com/ToyotaResearchInstitute/turbompc);摘要称成果已并入开源 TurboMPC 求解器,匿名 `git ls-remote` 验证存在;本文 SmoothedTurboMPC/FORL 训练代码是否已全部推送未逐项核对。
- 🇨🇳 可微 MPC 能端到端优化带约束的结构化策略,但面临两大瓶颈:活动集切换处灵敏度不光滑,以及每次策略评估都要求解一次优化,代价高昂。作者提出 SmoothedTurboMPC:GPU 并行的可微 MPC 求解器,外层 SQP、内层采用对数障碍平滑的 ADMM,在收敛时给出精确灵敏度且梯度平滑;再配合一阶强化学习(FORL)中的 SHAC 算法,复用 rollout 中的 MPC 解而不反复重新求解。与最先进求解器相比端到端训练吞吐最高提升 4.4 倍;在赛车任务上回报高于 MPC-PPO,速度快 46 倍,7 分钟内完成 330 万次转移的训练。最后用域随机化 RL 训练出更可靠的 MPC 赛车策略,并在全尺寸赛车上验证。
- 🇬🇧 Differentiable MPC enables end-to-end optimization of structured, constraint-aware policies, but suffers from nonsmooth sensitivities at active-set transitions and expensive per-evaluation solves. SmoothedTurboMPC is a GPU-parallel differentiable MPC solver combining SQP with a log-barrier-smoothed ADMM inner solver, giving smooth gradients and exact sensitivities at convergence; it is paired with first-order RL (SHAC), reusing rollout MPC solutions instead of re-solving. It achieves up to 4.4× higher end-to-end training throughput than state-of-the-art solvers and, on vehicle racing, higher returns than MPC-PPO with a 46× speedup (3.3M transitions in 7 minutes). A domain-randomized MPC racing policy trained this way is validated on a full-scale race car.

开源状态 / Code: 🕒 计划开源 / Planned — [项目页](https://robbyant-research.github.io/LHNav/)标注 "coming soon";页面链接的 [robbyant-research/LHNav](https://github.com/robbyant-research/LHNav) 目前只含项目页源文件(index.html、assets),无代码。

### [DreamVLA: From Generated Human Motions to Humanoid Vision-Language-Action Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10872) — 作者:Kevin Park, Izat Rasyad, Dvij Kalaria, Shankar Sastry 等

It is evaluated on platform picking, drawer opening, button pressing and walk-and-pick with ablations of DAgger iterations, residual RL and dataset scale; the abstract gives no quantitative results.

### [Burden, Bounty, or Bliss? Muscle-Aware Retargeting and Null-Space Exploration for Whole-Body Musculoskeletal Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10682) — 作者:Ruiyang Kong, Takeshi Oishi
开源状态 / Code: ❓ 未知 / Unknown — 投稿 ICRA 2027,无代码信息。
- 🇨🇳 从人体动作学习全身肌骨控制面临两难:参考轨迹运动学上合理却难以由肌肉驱动的动力学复现,且 700 维的冗余动作空间让无结构探索容易破坏目标动作。作者提出两阶段动作到策略流程:肌肉感知重定向(MAR)在语义、解剖与接触硬约束下对运动学轨迹做有界调整;零空间探索(NSE)估计肌肉兴奋到广义力的单帧映射,并沿其零空间向价值引导的流策略动作中注入有界、解剖结构化的扰动。在 24 段动捕基准上,MAR 与 NSE 结合可解出全部参考、以基线一半的样本达到完整时域,关节中心误差降低 44% 且无后期崩溃;作者称这是首个把单目视频转为可控制 700 块肌肉的全身模仿策略的端到端流程。
- 🇬🇧 Learning whole-body musculoskeletal control from human motion faces references that are kinematically plausible but hard to reproduce with muscles, and a redundant 700-dimensional action space where unstructured exploration disrupts the target motion. On MindCube it lifts Qwen3.5-27B from 52.70% to 71.14%, 7.52 points above pySpatial, and with identical annotated poses reaches 98.25% versus 71.81% for internal reasoning; it is also evaluated on SAT, MMSI-Bench and SpatialRGPT-Bench.

### [WorldMind: Test-Time Scaling for Visual Spatial Reasoning through Latent World Simulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10151) — 作者:Haoming Wang, Yongjian Fu, Ju Ren, Wei Gao
🏷️ 交叉方向 / Also: 世界模型 / World Model

- 🇨🇳 VLM 在需要"心理空间变换"(如假设视角或位置改变的换位思考)的任务上持续不足;现有方法要么扩展语言推理,要么给 VLM 增加静态几何表示,都不能主动变换所表示的场景;世界模型具备这一能力,但现有系统通过合成 RGB 图像通信,存在潜在-像素-潜在瓶颈。WorldMind 通过潜在世界模拟实现空间推理的测试时扩展:VLM 选择有用的空间变换,世界模型执行并把对齐的中间潜在特征直接暴露给 VLM 供后续推理使用,无需反复解码成像素;并采用过程可验证的强化学习,仅在相关动作与潜在转移有效时奖励推理进展。在 VSI-Bench、MMSI-Bench 与 MindCube-Tiny 上,相比基础 VLM 平均提升 16.0%,相比最强的 RGB 世界模拟方法提升 8.3%。

### [HIST3R: Decomposing and Rectifying State Updates with History for Streaming 3D Reconstruction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12011) — 作者:Kenneth Yang, Yu-Chiang Frank Wang
开源状态 / Code: 🕒 计划开源 / Planned — [项目页](https://kennethyangg.github.io/HIST3R/)标注 "coming soon";NeurIPS 2026(cs.CV 关键词检索补入)。
- 🇨🇳 带循环状态的流式 3D 重建能在有限内存下理解长视频,但把重建变成状态演化问题:每帧更新紧凑场景记忆,不可靠更新会覆盖历史上下文并累积为长时域几何漂移。作者用"方向-幅值"分解分析 CUT3R 式循环更新——幅值决定观测写入状态的强度,方向编码几何意图——发现现有免训练扩展只做幅值控制,能削弱但不能纠正不可靠更新的方向。HIST3R 基于 CUT3R、免训练地用历史同时控制两者:用局部一致的历史状态纠正更新方向,按当前观测与历史的视觉差异调节更新幅值,抑制冗余更新并保留互补证据。在位姿估计、3D 重建与视频深度上持续改善长时域表现,同时保持 CUT3R 的内存与速度;1000 帧输入时 ScanNet 与 TUM-Dynamics 上 ATE 分别比最强竞争方法降低 23.87% 与 33.65%,500 帧时 7-Scenes 与 NRGBD 中位重建精度比最强 CUT3R 变体提升 56.52% 与 33.96%。

Decomposing CUT3R-style updates into magnitude (write strength) and direction (geometric intent) shows existing training-free extensions only control magnitude. HIST3R, training-free on CUT3R, rectifies update direction with locally consistent historical states and modulates magnitude by visual discrepancy to history, keeping CUT3R's memory and speed. With 1,000 frames it cuts ATE by 23.87% on ScanNet and 33.65% on TUM-Dynamics versus the strongest competitor, and with 500 frames improves median accuracy over the best CUT3R variant by 56.52% on 7-Scenes and 33.96% on NRGBD.

### [PLS-GS: Probabilistic Lidar Scaffolds for Compact, Surface-Aligned Gaussian Splatting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10699) — 作者:Krishnan Harikumar, Alejandro Fontan, Thierry Peynot, Emili Hernández

- 🇨🇳 3DGS 通常只从图像优化,但许多机器人与测绘应用同时有相机与高精度激光雷达。PLS-GS 利用这一互补设置,构建与实测表面对齐的紧凑 3DGS:在位置与颜色上联合拟合分层高斯混合模型(GMM),得到连续自适应的支架,无需体素或八叉树等离散划分;每个渲染高斯关联一个 GMM 分量,并约束在该分量协方差导出的局部表面坐标系中,马氏支撑损失限制其偏离 LiDAR 表面的程度。在五个大型室外场景中,PLS-GS 的基元比图像初始化和 LiDAR 初始化基线都更贴近 LiDAR 测量,同时使用的基元少得多,模型更紧凑、渲染更快;同等容量下渲染质量相当。

On five large outdoor scenes it places primitives much closer to LiDAR measurements than image- and LiDAR-initialized baselines with far fewer primitives, giving a more compact model and faster rendering at comparable quality.
